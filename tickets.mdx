**Tickets** is a desk-style queue for issues that need an owner and status. Replies here are **free text** typed by your staff.

<Note>
  Tickets may require a plan upgrade. Bot reply wording is edited in [Message templates](/message-templates), not in Tickets.
</Note>

## Reply to a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tickets">
    In the system sidebar under Messaging, click **Tickets**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the ticket">
    Filter by All / Open / Answered / Closed. Search by username, ticket ID, or subject. Use unread or date filters when available.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the thread">
    Review messages, attachments, assignee, and status.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send a reply">
    Type your response in the composer and send. If the ticket is locked, you will see that it cannot be replied to until unlocked.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Use [Inbox](/inbox) for live channel chats that are not ticket threads.

## Sync from Perfect Panel

1. Use **Sync** on the Tickets page when you need to pull the latest tickets from Perfect Panel.
2. Watch sync progress in the UI.
3. If a sync run is stuck, use **Reset sync**, then try again.

Keep **SMM Panel** credentials healthy — sync depends on a working panel connection. See [Connect Perfect Panel](/panel/panel-api-settings).

## Ticket settings

Open **Ticket settings** from the Tickets page to adjust high-level options such as:

- How often tickets sync
- How far back to look when syncing
- The staff name shown on replies

Save changes before leaving the dialog.
