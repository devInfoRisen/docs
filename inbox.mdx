**Inbox** is for live conversations on your connected channels. Use it when a customer needs a human in the moment.

## Reply to a chat

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Inbox">
    In the system sidebar under Messaging, click **Inbox**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select a conversation">
    Pick a thread from the list. Use filters to narrow by channel or status when available.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review the customer">
    Check the side panel for linked username and other details before promising order changes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send a reply">
    Type your message and send on the same channel the customer used.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Resume AI

If AI was handling the chat and you took over, use **Resume AI** (when shown) after you finish so the bot can continue for later messages.

## When to use Inbox vs Tickets

| Use **Inbox** when… | Use **Tickets** when… |
|---|---|
| Quick live chat | The issue needs status and an owner |
| Channel DM / widget thread | Follow-up across a longer period |

See [Tickets](/tickets).
