## Providers

**Providers** stores upstream supplier contacts used when refill or speed-up work needs a human on the supplier side.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Providers">
    In the system sidebar under Integrations, click **Providers**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add or edit a provider">
    Map the supplier to WhatsApp and/or Telegram contacts as shown in the form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Test">
    Send a test message when the UI offers it, so you know the contact can receive alerts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Keep contacts up to date when suppliers change numbers.

## Orders Tracker

**Orders Tracker** helps you look up Perfect Panel order progress from the system panel.

1. Open **Orders Tracker** under Integrations.
2. Search or open the order you need.
3. Follow up in Inbox, Tickets, or with the provider as needed.

<Note>
  Orders Tracker may require a plan upgrade.
</Note>

## Request Report

**Request Report** shows history of order-related requests and follow-ups from customers.

1. Open **Request Report** under Integrations.
2. Filter by date or status as available.
3. Use the list for ops review — keep exports inside your team.

<Note>
  Request Report may require a plan upgrade.
</Note>
