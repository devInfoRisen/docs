Every customer who wants to use Nexa's bot commands must have their WhatsApp or Telegram number linked to their Perfect Panel username in your Nexa dashboard. Until you create that link, Nexa has no way to verify who the sender is, so any commands they send will be automatically denied. Linking is a one-time step you perform as the operator — customers cannot register themselves.

## How Linking Works

When a customer sends a command, Nexa looks up their phone number in your customer list and retrieves the Perfect Panel username it's associated with. It then checks Perfect Panel to confirm the referenced order belongs to that username. The link you create here is the foundation of the entire ownership verification process.

<Info>
  Customers cannot self-register. You must add each customer manually or via bulk import. This keeps control in your hands and prevents unauthorised accounts from gaining access.
</Info>

## Add a Customer

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Customers section">
    In the Nexa dashboard, navigate to **Customers** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add or locate the customer">
    Click **Add Customer** to create a new entry. If the customer already exists in your list, search for them by name or phone number and open their record.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter their phone number and panel username">
    Fill in the customer's WhatsApp or Telegram phone number (in international format, e.g. `+447911123456`) and their exact Perfect Panel username. Double-check both values — a mismatch will cause verification failures for that customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the record">
    Click **Save**. The customer is now linked and verified. From this point forward, commands they send from that number will be matched against their Perfect Panel account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Unlinked Senders Experience

If someone sends a command from a number that isn't in your customer list, Nexa replies with a message explaining that their number hasn't been verified and that they should contact support. No order data is exposed and no action is taken. This applies equally to typos in a number, numbers that were removed, and completely unknown senders.

<Tip>
  If you have a large existing customer base, use the **bulk import** feature in the Customers section. Prepare a CSV file with columns for phone number and Perfect Panel username, then upload it to link all your customers in a single step. This saves significant time during initial setup.
</Tip>

## Things to Keep in Mind

<Warning>
  Phone numbers must be entered in international format including the country code (e.g. `+1`, `+44`, `+91`). Numbers entered without a country code will not match incoming messages and verification will always fail for that customer.
</Warning>

- **One number per customer.** Each phone number can only be linked to a single Perfect Panel username. If a customer changes their number, update their record in the dashboard.
- **Customers cannot self-register.** The operator is always responsible for adding and managing customer records. This is by design — it keeps the linking process authoritative and prevents abuse.
- **Removing a customer** unlinks their number immediately. Any subsequent commands from that number will be denied until you re-add them.
