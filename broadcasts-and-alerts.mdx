## Broadcast

**Broadcast** sends a one-time message to customers or groups on your connected channels.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Broadcast">
    In the system sidebar under Messaging, click **Broadcast**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compose">
    Write the message. Use the **Templates** tab if you keep reusable multi-language broadcast copy.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose the audience">
    Apply audience filters and check the preview count before sending.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send or schedule">
    Send now, or schedule for later. Cancel a scheduled broadcast from the list if you need to stop it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Auto Alert

**Auto Alert** posts updates to channels or groups you manage (for example service or status notices).

1. Open **Auto Alert** under Integrations.
2. Configure targets and message settings in the UI.
3. Use scan / queue / test actions when available before relying on it in production.

<Note>
  Broadcast is campaign-style messaging. Auto Alert is for ongoing operational posts.
</Note>
