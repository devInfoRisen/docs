Nexa's AI support module sits inside your bot and handles incoming customer questions automatically — no manual replies needed for routine inquiries. When a customer asks about their order status, your panel's services, or anything else you've documented, the AI searches your knowledge base and responds instantly through the same WhatsApp or Telegram channel they already use. You stay focused on running your panel while your customers get answers any time of day.

## How It Works

Every incoming message passes through Nexa's routing layer. Command messages — such as `refill`, `partial`, or `speed up` — are handled by the command engine. General questions are routed to the AI support module, which follows this flow:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Customer Sends a Question">
    A customer messages your bot on WhatsApp or Telegram asking, for example, about delivery times or how to request a refill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="AI Searches the Knowledge Base">
    Nexa queries your knowledge base articles and FAQs to find the most relevant answer for that question.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confident Answer → Replies Automatically">
    If the AI's confidence score meets your threshold, it sends the answer directly to the customer with no intervention from you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Not Confident → Creates a Support Ticket">
    If the AI cannot find a suitable answer, it creates a support ticket in your Nexa dashboard so you can follow up manually.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Command messages and AI support messages use the same bot connection but are handled by separate engines. A customer can send a `refill order 123` command and a general question in the same conversation — each message is routed correctly without any configuration on your part.
</Note>
