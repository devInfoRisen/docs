Welcome to Nexa — the automation layer that connects your SMM panel to WhatsApp and Telegram so your customers can manage their own orders without ever opening a ticket. Instead of fielding repetitive support messages, you configure Nexa once and let verified customers send commands like `refill order 123` straight from their chat app. Nexa checks ownership, talks to Perfect Panel on their behalf, and replies instantly.

## Get up and running in four steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your Panel API">
    Go to **Panel API Settings** and enter your Perfect Panel base URL and API key so Nexa can read and act on orders.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Channel">
    Navigate to **Channels** and connect a WhatsApp account (QR scan) or a Telegram bot (webhook token) that your customers will message.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link customer accounts">
    In **Customers**, verify that each WhatsApp or Telegram number belongs to the correct panel username — Nexa won't act on any command until this link is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customers send commands">
    Once linked, customers send messages like `refill order 123` directly in chat. Nexa verifies ownership, calls Perfect Panel, and replies with a confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>
