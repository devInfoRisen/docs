The `cancel order` command is designed to let a verified customer request cancellation of an order without contacting support. Nexa will verify that the sender owns the order and then submit a cancellation request to Perfect Panel on the customer's behalf. The feature is fully specified and on the roadmap — it is not yet available in the current release.

<Warning>
  **Coming Soon — not yet live.** The `cancel order` command is a planned feature and is **not currently available**. Sending this command will not cancel your order at this time. Please contact support directly to request a cancellation while you wait for this feature to ship.
</Warning>

## Syntax

```text
cancel order <ID>
```

Replace `<ID>` with the numeric order ID from your Perfect Panel dashboard.

**Example:**

```text
cancel order 5381
```

## Intended Behaviour

When the `cancel order` command goes live, here is how it will work:

1. **Customer sends the command.** The customer types `cancel order <ID>` in the Nexa bot conversation on WhatsApp or Telegram.
2. **Nexa verifies ownership.** Nexa checks that the sender's number is verified for a Perfect Panel username and that the order belongs to that same username. If either check fails, the request is denied and the customer receives an explanation.
3. **Nexa submits the cancellation.** Once ownership is confirmed, Nexa forwards a cancellation request to Perfect Panel. The outcome depends on whether the order is in a cancellable state — Perfect Panel's own cancellation rules apply.
4. **Customer receives a confirmation.** Nexa replies in chat to confirm the cancellation has been submitted, or explains why it couldn't be processed.

## In the Meantime

Until the `cancel order` command is live, customers who need to cancel an order should reach out to your support team directly. Your team can submit the cancellation manually through the Perfect Panel dashboard.

<Tip>
  Let your customers know your preferred support contact — whether that's a WhatsApp number, a Telegram group, or a helpdesk link — so they can get cancellations processed quickly while this feature is in development.
</Tip>
