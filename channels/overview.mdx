A **Channel** is a connected messaging account — either a WhatsApp number or a Telegram bot — through which your customers send automation commands and receive replies. Every message that Nexa processes flows through a Channel: when a customer sends `refill order 123` in a chat, Nexa receives that message via the Channel, verifies ownership with Perfect Panel, executes the action, and responds — all without manual intervention. Connecting at least one Channel is the first step to putting Nexa to work for your SMM panel.

## Supported Channel Types

Nexa supports two messaging platforms. Choose the one that best fits your customer base, or connect both to maximise reach.

## Multiple Channels per Space

Each Nexa Space supports **multiple Channels at the same time**. You can, for example, run one WhatsApp number for your English-speaking customers and a separate Telegram bot for a different market — both feeding into the same Space, the same automation rules, and the same Perfect Panel integration. Add as many Channels as your operation requires; there is no fixed limit imposed by Nexa itself.

## Managing Your Channels

After you connect a Channel, you can monitor and control it from the **Channels** section of your Space dashboard.

### Viewing Channel Status

Each Channel card displays a live status badge:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | The Channel is connected and processing messages normally. |
| **Inactive** | The Channel is connected but has been disabled; messages are not processed. |
| **Disconnected** | The session has dropped (WhatsApp) or the webhook is unreachable (Telegram); reconnection is required. |

### Enabling and Disabling a Channel

Toggle a Channel between Active and Inactive without fully removing it. This is useful when you want to pause a number for maintenance or seasonal downtime without losing its configuration.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Channel list">
    In your Space, navigate to **Channels** in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Locate the Channel">
    Find the Channel you want to enable or disable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the status switch">
    Click the **Active / Inactive** toggle on the Channel card. The status badge updates immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Disconnecting a Channel

Disconnecting permanently removes the Channel's session or webhook registration from Nexa. The messaging account itself (your phone number or Telegram bot) is unaffected.

<Warning>
  Disconnecting a Channel stops all message processing for that account immediately. Customers who message it will receive no automated response until you reconnect.
</Warning>

To disconnect, open the Channel's detail view and click **Disconnect Channel**, then confirm the prompt. You can reconnect the same account as a new Channel at any time.
