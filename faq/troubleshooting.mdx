Most issues with Nexa fall into a handful of categories: commands not responding, a channel going offline, or a broken API connection. Work through the relevant section below to identify and fix the problem. Each issue includes the most likely cause and the steps to resolve it.

## Commands Not Working

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My customer sent a command but got no reply">
    Start with the two most common causes:

    1. **Check that the channel is connected and active.** Go to **Channels** in your Nexa dashboard and confirm the WhatsApp or Telegram channel your customer is using shows a **Connected** status. If it shows **Disconnected**, follow the steps in the [WhatsApp channel disconnected](#whatsapp-channel-disconnected) or [Telegram bot not receiving messages](#telegram-bot-not-receiving-messages) sections below.

    2. **Check that the customer's number is linked.** Go to **Contacts** and search for the customer's phone number. If they don't appear or their account shows as unlinked, they need to complete the account linking process before commands will work.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Command denied: ownership verification failed">
    This error means Nexa found the customer's linked username but the order ID they provided does not belong to that username in Perfect Panel.

    - Open Perfect Panel and look up the order ID the customer sent.
    - Check the **Customer** field on that order and confirm it matches the username linked to the customer's phone number in Nexa.
    - If the names don't match, the customer is attempting a command on someone else's order — the denial is correct and expected.
    - If the names should match, check whether the customer's phone number is linked to the right username in Nexa under **Contacts**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Refill command says order is not eligible">
    The refill command only works on orders that have a **Completed** status in Perfect Panel. If the order is still **Pending**, **In Progress**, or **Partial**, the refill request will be rejected.

    - Open Perfect Panel and check the current status of the order ID the customer provided.
    - If the order is not yet complete, let the customer know they need to wait until it finishes before requesting a refill.
    - If the order shows as **Completed** in Perfect Panel but Nexa still rejects it, verify that your Panel API connection is working correctly under **Settings → Panel API**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Channel Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="WhatsApp channel disconnected">
    WhatsApp connections use a QR-based session that must stay active on a physical or virtual device. A disconnection usually means the session expired or the device went offline.

    1. Go to **Channels** in your Nexa dashboard and open the disconnected WhatsApp channel.
    2. Click **Reconnect** to generate a new QR code.
    3. Open WhatsApp on the phone associated with this channel, go to **Linked Devices**, and scan the new QR code.
    4. Confirm the channel status changes to **Connected** in the Nexa dashboard.

    <Warning>
      Keep the phone running WhatsApp online at all times. If the device loses power, goes into deep sleep, or loses its internet connection for an extended period, the WhatsApp session will disconnect again and you'll need to re-scan.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Telegram bot not receiving messages">
    Nexa registers a webhook with Telegram to receive messages. If the webhook registration failed, your bot will appear online in Telegram but won't respond to any messages.

    1. Go to **Channels** and open your Telegram channel.
    2. Look for an error banner — Nexa displays a webhook error message if registration failed.
    3. Verify that the **Bot Token** field contains the correct token from [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather). Even a single incorrect character will cause registration to fail.
    4. Correct the token if needed and click **Save**. Nexa will re-attempt webhook registration automatically.
    5. Send a test message to your bot and confirm it receives a reply.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Panel API Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="API connection test failed">
    A failed connection test means Nexa cannot reach your Perfect Panel using the credentials you provided. The two most common causes are a malformed URL and an incorrect API key.

    1. Go to **Settings → Panel API** in your Nexa dashboard.
    2. Check the **Base URL** field:
       - It must start with `https://` (not `http://`).
       - It must **not** end with a trailing slash — use `https://mypanel.com`, not `https://mypanel.com/`.
    3. Check the **API Key** field:
       - Copy the key directly from your Perfect Panel admin area to avoid typos.
       - Confirm that the **Admin API** is enabled in Perfect Panel's settings — the connection will fail if it's disabled.
    4. Click **Test Connection** again. If the test passes, save your settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see recent commands in Reports">
    The Reports section filters by date range. If commands appear to be missing, the most likely explanation is that they fall outside the currently selected window.

    1. Go to **Reports** in your Nexa dashboard.
    2. Open the **Date Range** filter and widen the window to include the period you're looking for.
    3. Apply the filter and check whether the missing commands appear.

    If you've confirmed the date range is correct and commands are still missing, check that the relevant channel was **Connected** during that period — commands sent while a channel was disconnected are not logged.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  If you've worked through the steps above and the issue is still happening, open a support ticket directly from your Nexa dashboard under **Tickets → New Ticket**, or contact the Nexa support team. Include the affected channel, the command or action that failed, and any error messages you saw — this helps the team resolve your issue faster.
</Tip>
