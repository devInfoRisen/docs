## Account

1. Use a unique password for [https://smmpanel-ai.com](https://smmpanel-ai.com).
2. Enable two-factor authentication in Account **Settings**.
3. Review sessions and revoke devices you do not recognize.

## System

1. Give staff the minimum roles they need under **Roles & Permissions** and **Team members**.
2. Rotate Perfect Panel Admin API keys if someone leaves or a key leaks — update **SMM Panel** right away.
3. Treat WhatsApp linked devices as sensitive access.
4. Use **Trusted Senders** sparingly.

## Space settings

Under **Settings → Space** (when shown), update the system name, logo, and related display options. Only owners or admins should change these.

## Staff alerts

Under **Settings → Staff alerts**, choose where staff should receive operational alerts. Keep destinations limited to people who need them.

## Ownership

Do not try to bypass ownership checks. They protect your customers.
