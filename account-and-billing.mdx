Your **Account** at [https://smmpanel-ai.com](https://smmpanel-ai.com) is where you manage systems, money, and invites. Day-to-day support happens inside each system panel.

## Signup and login

1. Register at [https://smmpanel-ai.com](https://smmpanel-ai.com).
2. Verify your email.
3. Sign in to **My Systems**.
4. Optional: enable two-factor authentication in **Settings**.

Team members invited to a system sign in to that panel only - they do not see Account billing.

## My Systems

From **My Systems** you can:

- Launch a new system (name, plan, pay if required)
- Open an existing panel
- Invite team members for a system
- Buy AI credits for a system when needed

## Wallet and plans

1. Open **Billing** in your Account.
2. Deposit to your wallet when you need balance.
3. Use wallet balance to pay plan invoices when you launch or renew a system.

## AI credits

AI features on a system may need AI credits. Buy credits from the system actions on **My Systems** when replies stop because credit is low.

## Invite team

1. Open **Team** for a system from My Systems.
2. Invite staff by email.
3. Inside the panel, assign roles under **Administration** so people only see what they need.
