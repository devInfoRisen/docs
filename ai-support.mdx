**AI Support** helps with general customer questions. Keep answers accurate by maintaining your knowledge base and reply templates.

<Note>
  AI Support may require a plan upgrade. Hosted usage may consume AI credits.
</Note>

## Get started

1. Open **AI Support** in the system sidebar.
2. Enable or configure the agent if your plan includes it.
3. Add short knowledge articles for common questions (delivery times, refill rules, contact info).
4. Review prompts or tone settings when available.
5. Test with a few sample questions before relying on it live.

## Reply templates

Under AI Support, open **Reply templates** (or the equivalent section) to edit fixed AI replies such as:

- Payment-related answers
- Handoff to a human
- Off-topic replies

Save each template after editing. These are separate from [Message templates](/message-templates) (bot command / system copy).

## When AI should hand off

Escalate to a human ([Inbox](/inbox) or [Tickets](/tickets)) when:

- The question needs account-specific judgment
- The customer is upset or stuck
- The topic is not covered in your knowledge base

In Inbox, use **Resume AI** after a human finishes if you want the bot to continue later.

## Credits

If AI stops answering, check that AI is enabled and that AI credits remain under Account → My Systems. See [Account and billing](/account-and-billing).
