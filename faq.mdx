## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is SMMPANEL AI?">
    A support platform for SMM panel operators. Connect your panel and chat channels, then help customers with common order requests while your team uses Inbox and tickets.
  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where is billing vs support chat?">
  Billing and My Systems live in your Account at
  [https://smmpanel-ai.com](https://smmpanel-ai.com). Day-to-day chat tools live
  inside each system panel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why was a command denied?">
  Usually the chat identity is not linked to the right username, or the order
  does not belong to that username. Check **Customers**, then confirm the order
  owner in Perfect Panel.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I use WhatsApp and Telegram together?">
  Yes. Connect both under **Channels** on the same system if your plan allows.
</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is self-hosted documented here?">
    No. Self-hosted is available on request - email [hello@smmpanel-ai.com](mailto:hello@smmpanel-ai.com).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I edit bot languages?">
    Open **Message Templates**, then **Language settings** (gear). Enable or disable languages there. See [Message templates](/message-templates).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Where do I edit ticket replies vs bot messages?">
    Staff ticket replies are typed free text in **Tickets**. Bot wording is in **Message Templates**. AI fixed replies are in **AI Support → Reply templates**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I let AI continue after I replied in Inbox?">
    Open the conversation in **Inbox** and use **Resume AI** when it is shown. See [Inbox](/inbox).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Customer got no reply">
    1. Confirm the channel is active under **Channels**.
    2. Confirm the customer is linked under **Customers**.
    3. Confirm **SMM Panel** credentials still pass the connection test.
  </Accordion>

<Accordion title="Panel connection test fails">
  Check the base URL (https, no trailing slash) and that you pasted an Admin API
  key. Save and test again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="WhatsApp disconnected">
  Open the WhatsApp channel and scan a new QR code from the phone that should
  stay linked.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Telegram bot silent">
  Confirm the bot token is still valid in BotFather. Update or recreate the
  Telegram channel in SMMPANEL AI.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="AI stopped answering">
  Check that AI is enabled for the system and that AI credits remain. See
  [Account and billing](/account-and-billing).
</Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Team member cannot see billing">
    Expected. Team members only access the system panel. Account owners manage billing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

Still stuck? Email [hello@smmpanel-ai.com](mailto:hello@smmpanel-ai.com) with the channel, what you tried, and any error text you saw.
