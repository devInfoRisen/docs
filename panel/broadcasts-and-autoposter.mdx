Nexa gives you two ways to push messages outward to your customer base: **Broadcasts** for one-time bulk sends, and **Auto-poster** for recurring scheduled posts. Both features operate through your connected WhatsApp and Telegram channels, so the messages your customers receive look like they come directly from the channel they already use to place orders — keeping communication consistent and on-brand.

---

## Broadcasts

A broadcast lets you send a single message to all of your customers, or to a filtered subset, through any connected channel. Use broadcasts for order updates, planned downtime notices, pricing changes, or promotional offers.

### Send a Broadcast

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Broadcasts">
    In the Nexa sidebar, click **Broadcasts**. The Broadcasts dashboard lists all past and scheduled broadcasts with their delivery status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a channel">
    Click **New Broadcast** and choose the channel you want to send from — either a WhatsApp number or a Telegram bot/channel. Only channels with an active connection appear in this list. If a channel is missing, check its status under [Channels](/channels/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compose your message">
    Type your message in the composer. You can use the following formatting:

    | Syntax | Output |
    |--------|--------|
    | `*text*` | **Bold** (WhatsApp) |
    | `_text_` | _Italic_ (WhatsApp) |
    | `**text**` | **Bold** (Telegram) |
    | `[variable]` | Personalisation token (e.g. `[first_name]`) |

    Attach an image, video, or document using the paperclip icon if your message needs a visual element.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your recipients">
    Select who receives this broadcast:

    <AccordionGroup>
      <Accordion title="All Customers">
        Every customer registered in your Nexa account across all channels. This is the broadest send option.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Specific List">
        Upload a CSV of phone numbers or Telegram IDs, or select a previously saved customer list.
      </Accordion>
      <Accordion title="Filter by Tag">
        Send only to customers who have a specific tag applied in [Customers](/verification/linking-accounts) — for example, `vip`, `reseller`, or `inactive-30d`.
      </Accordion>
    </AccordionGroup>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule or send immediately">
    Choose **Send Now** to dispatch the broadcast straight away, or toggle **Schedule** and pick a date and time to queue the send for later. Scheduled broadcasts appear in the Broadcasts list with a `scheduled` badge and can be edited or cancelled before they go out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Broadcast Delivery Status

After sending, the Broadcasts list shows a delivery summary for each campaign.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `sent` | Message dispatched to the channel API |
| `delivered` | Confirmed receipt by the recipient's device (WhatsApp only) |
| `read` | Recipient opened the message (WhatsApp only) |
| `failed` | Delivery failed — usually due to a blocked number or channel disconnect |

## Auto-poster

Auto-poster lets you schedule recurring posts to a connected channel on a set cadence. It is ideal for automated status updates (e.g. "All services are running normally ✅"), weekly promotions, or any content you want to push on a regular basis without manual effort.

### Set Up an Auto-poster Post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Auto-poster">
    In the Nexa sidebar, click **Auto-poster**. The Auto-poster list shows all active and paused recurring posts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new post">
    Click **New Post**. Select the channel to post to and compose your message content in the editor. As with Broadcasts, you can include text formatting and media attachments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the schedule">
    Configure when and how often the post goes out:

    | Setting | Options |
    |---------|---------|
    | **Start date** | The date of the first post |
    | **Time** | The time of day (in your account timezone) |
    | **Frequency** | Once, Daily, Weekly, Bi-weekly, Monthly |
    | **End date** | Optional — leave blank to run indefinitely |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate the post">
    Click **Activate**. The post status changes to `active` and Nexa will send it automatically at each scheduled interval. You can pause or delete a post at any time from the Auto-poster list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Messaging Frequency and Spam

<Warning>
  WhatsApp and Telegram both enforce messaging policies that can get your channel restricted or banned if you send too many messages too quickly. Follow these guidelines to protect your channels:

  - **Do not broadcast to cold lists.** Only message customers who have opted in by initiating a conversation with your channel first.
  - **Respect frequency limits.** Avoid sending more than one broadcast per day to the same recipients.
  - **Avoid spammy content.** All-caps text, excessive emojis, unsolicited promotions, and misleading claims increase the chance of your messages being reported.
  - **Monitor failure rates.** A high `failed` rate in your Broadcasts list can be an early warning sign that your channel is being throttled. Pause sends and review your channel health in [Channels](/channels/overview) if this happens.
</Warning>
