The Nexa Dashboard is your central command centre. Every time you log in, it gives you an immediate read on the health of your automation — what's running, what's failing, and where your customers are most active. Use it as your first stop before diving into any specific panel section.

## Key Metrics Cards

The top of the Dashboard displays four at-a-glance metric cards that update in real time.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Total Customers" icon="users">
    The total number of customer accounts registered across all your connected channels. Tap through to the Customers section for a full breakdown.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Commands Today" icon="terminal">
    A running count of every refill, partial, speed-up, and cancel command processed in the current calendar day. Spikes here can indicate a busy promotion or an unusual usage pattern.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Active Channels" icon="satellite-dish">
    The number of WhatsApp and Telegram channels currently connected and accepting commands. A drop in this number means a channel has disconnected and needs attention in [Channels](/channels/overview).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Open Tickets" icon="ticket">
    Unresolved support tickets raised by your customers. Keep this number low by routing common queries through your [AI Support & Knowledge Base](/ai-support/overview) before they become tickets.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Recent Activity Feed

Below the metric cards, the Dashboard shows a live feed of the most recent events across your panel.

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Time** | Timestamp of the event (your account timezone) |
| **Customer** | The customer who triggered the action |
| **Channel** | Which WhatsApp or Telegram channel was used |
| **Command** | The command type — refill, partial, speed-up, cancel, etc. |
| **Status** | `success`, `pending`, `denied`, or `error` |

Click any row to jump directly to the full command record in [Reports](/panel/reports).

## Spotting Unusual Activity

<Tip>
  Check the Dashboard **Commands Today** card against your typical daily average. A sudden spike — especially in `denied` or `error` statuses — often signals a misconfigured customer account, an expired API key, or an attempt to abuse your panel. Drill into [Reports → Command History](/panel/reports#command-history) to investigate individual records quickly.
</Tip>
