The Reports section gives you a detailed, searchable record of everything that has happened across your Nexa account. Whether you need to investigate a failing command, prove a refill was triggered, identify your most active customers, or measure channel performance over a given period, Reports is where you go. Every action Nexa takes is logged automatically — you do not need to enable anything for data collection to start.

## Access Reports

In the Nexa sidebar, click **Reports**. The Reports page opens on the **Command History** tab by default. Use the tabs along the top to switch between report types, and the date range picker in the top-right corner to scope the data to the period you need.

---

## Report Types

### Command History

Command History records every command your customers have sent through Nexa — refills, partial refills, speed-ups, cancellations, and any other supported actions — along with the outcome of each one.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Timestamp** | Date and time the command was received |
| **Customer** | The customer account that sent the command |
| **Channel** | The WhatsApp or Telegram channel used |
| **Command** | Command type: `refill`, `partial`, `speed-up`, `cancel` |
| **Order ID** | The Perfect Panel order reference the command targeted |
| **Status** | `success`, `pending`, `denied`, or `error` |
| **Details** | Expanded reason for `denied` or `error` outcomes |

Click any row to open the full command record, which includes the original message text, the API response from Perfect Panel, and any error codes returned.

---

### Order Activity

Order Activity groups all commands by the orders they acted on, making it easier to see the full lifecycle of a specific order at a glance — rather than finding individual entries scattered through Command History.

Filter the view by:
- **Action type** — refill, partial, speed-up, cancel
- **Time frame** — today, this week, this month, custom range
- **Status** — successful actions only, or include failed ones

This report is useful when a customer disputes whether a refill was actually triggered. You can pull up the exact order ID and show the timestamped log of every action taken against it.

---

### Customer Activity

Customer Activity ranks your customers by their command volume over the selected date range and surfaces any linked accounts.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Customer** | Customer name and contact identifier |
| **Commands** | Total commands sent in the period |
| **Last Active** | Date and time of their most recent command |
| **Channels** | Which channels they use |
| **Linked Accounts** | Other accounts associated with the same user |

Use this report to identify your highest-volume customers (who may benefit from a VIP tag or custom limits) and to spot duplicate or linked accounts that could indicate shared credentials or suspicious behaviour.

---

### Channel Performance

Channel Performance shows messaging activity broken down by each of your connected WhatsApp and Telegram channels.

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Messages Sent** | Total outbound messages sent by Nexa through this channel |
| **Messages Received** | Total inbound messages received from customers |
| **Commands Processed** | Number of valid commands handled |
| **Error Rate** | Percentage of commands that returned `error` or `denied` |
| **Uptime** | Percentage of the selected period the channel was connected |

A low uptime figure combined with a high error rate typically means the channel disconnected partway through the period. Check the channel's connection status under [Channels](/channels/overview).

## Date Range Filtering

Every report type supports flexible date filtering.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the date picker">
    Click the date range selector in the top-right corner of the Reports page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a preset or custom range">
    Select a preset (**Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **This month**, **Last month**) or click **Custom Range** to pick a specific start and end date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply the filter">
    Click **Apply**. All four report tabs update to show data only within the selected range.
  </Step>
</Steps>

---

## Export Options

To export any report, click the **Export** button in the top-right corner of the relevant tab. Nexa generates a CSV file containing all rows matching your current filters (including the date range). The file downloads to your browser immediately.

<Note>
  Exports are limited to 10,000 rows per file. If your date range produces more rows than this, narrow the range and export in multiple batches.
</Note>

## Identifying Misconfigured Accounts

<Tip>
  Open **Command History**, filter by **Status → error** or **Status → denied**, and sort by **Customer**. If the same customer appears repeatedly with `denied` or `error` results, their account is likely misconfigured — for example, their linked Perfect Panel user ID is wrong, or they are sending commands for orders they do not own. Reach out to them proactively or adjust their permissions in [Customers](/verification/linking-accounts) before the failures escalate into support tickets.
</Tip>
