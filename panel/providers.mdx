A provider is an upstream supplier that fulfils the social media services your customers order through your panel. When a customer triggers a refill or speed-up command in Nexa, the platform can automatically notify the relevant provider — either by sending a message to a WhatsApp number or by posting into a shared WhatsApp Support Group. Keeping your provider list accurate ensures those notifications reach the right people without any manual forwarding on your part.

## Add a Provider

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Providers">
    In the Nexa sidebar, click **Providers**. The Providers list displays all suppliers you have already configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Add Provider">
    Click the **Add Provider** button in the top-right corner of the page. A configuration panel slides open on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter provider details">
    Fill in the following fields:

    | Field | What to enter |
    |-------|---------------|
    | **Provider Name** | A recognisable label for this supplier (e.g. *Provider A – Instagram*) |
    | **Contact Type** | Choose **WhatsApp Number** or **WhatsApp Group** |
    | **WhatsApp Number / Group** | The full international phone number (e.g. `+447911123456`) or the group invite link |

    If you are routing notifications through a Support Group rather than a direct number, select **WhatsApp Group** as the contact type and choose the relevant group from the dropdown. See [Support Groups](#support-groups) below for details on setting those up first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Associate with a Support Group (optional)">
    If this provider should receive notifications inside a WhatsApp group, open the **Support Group** dropdown and select the group you have already configured. Nexa will send all refill and speed-up alerts for this provider directly into that group thread.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the provider">
    Click **Save**. The new provider appears in your Providers list and is immediately available for association with your services.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Support Groups

A Support Group is a WhatsApp group that Nexa joins and uses to deliver provider notifications automatically. Instead of Nexa messaging a provider one-to-one, it posts into the shared group — where you and the provider are both participants — so the full conversation history is visible to everyone involved.

### Set Up a Support Group

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Support Groups">
    In the Nexa sidebar, click **Support Groups**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the Nexa bot to your WhatsApp group">
    Copy the Nexa bot's WhatsApp number shown on the Support Groups page and add it as a participant to your existing provider WhatsApp group. Nexa requires group admin rights to post messages reliably — grant admin status to the bot after adding it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the group in Nexa">
    Click **Add Support Group**, enter a name for the group, and paste in the group's WhatsApp link or ID. Nexa will verify it can post to the group.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the group to a provider">
    Return to **Providers**, open the relevant provider record, and select this group from the **Support Group** dropdown.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Each Support Group can be shared by multiple providers if they all communicate through the same WhatsApp group. However, using separate groups per provider gives you a cleaner notification history and reduces the risk of missed messages in a busy thread.
</Note>

## Service Tracker

The Service Tracker monitors live order statuses across your providers and surfaces delays before your customers notice them. Rather than manually checking each provider's dashboard, you get a single view inside Nexa.

### What the Service Tracker Shows

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Order ID** | The Perfect Panel order reference |
| **Service** | The service type (e.g. Instagram followers, YouTube views) |
| **Provider** | The upstream supplier fulfilling the order |
| **Status** | Current order state: `active`, `partial`, `slow`, `stalled`, or `complete` |
| **Last Updated** | Timestamp of the most recent status check |

### Reading the Status Indicators

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Active">
    The order is progressing normally. No action is needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Partial">
    The order has partially completed and has stopped progressing. This typically means the provider ran out of supply. Consider triggering a refill or contacting the provider through their Support Group.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Slow">
    The order is still active but delivery speed has dropped below the expected rate. Nexa flags this automatically based on the expected delivery window you configured for the service.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Stalled">
    No progress has been recorded for an extended period. Nexa has already sent an automatic alert to the provider's Support Group if that is configured. Review the order and follow up manually if needed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Complete">
    The order has been delivered in full.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Tip>
  Sort the Service Tracker by **Status** and filter for `stalled` or `slow` orders at the start of each day. Addressing these proactively prevents customer refill requests from piling up and keeps your panel's completion rate healthy.
</Tip>
